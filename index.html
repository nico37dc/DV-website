Project

Artificial intelligence has moved from research labs into everyday business software: chatbots answer customers, machine-learning models forecast demand and text-generation tools draft documents. For European enterprises the question is no longer whether AI exists, but whether and how they put it to work.

The guiding question of this website is: how widely do European enterprises use AI, and how does this differ between countries, company sizes and years? The answers are based on official statistics from Eurostat, which surveys enterprises with at least 10 employees in all EU member states every year.

The visualizations below compare countries, show how adoption depends on the size of an enterprise, follow its development over time and look at how many different AI technologies enterprises combine.

AI adoption in European enterprises

How many European enterprises use AI?

Eurostat counts an enterprise as an AI user if it uses at least one AI technology, for example text mining, speech recognition, text or image generation, image recognition, machine learning or AI-based process automation. All figures refer to enterprises with at least 10 employees (excluding the financial sector) and are given as a percentage of all enterprises in the group.

The survey was carried out in 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025; there is no data for 2022. Hover over (or tap) any bar, cell or square to see the exact value.

Note: a few values are flagged by Eurostat as a break in the time series (France 2023, Sweden 2023, Netherlands 2025); they are marked in the tooltips. Data downloaded from the Eurostat API in November 2026 and prepared with scripts/prepare_data.py.
